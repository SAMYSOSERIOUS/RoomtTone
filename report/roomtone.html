    The 70,345 events behind this page were generated by Roomtone's simulator to look like a real stream: 2,400 people in four languages who sleep and sometimes delete, 300 scripts in three styles that sleep in their operators' time zones, 60 declared helper bots, and 120 sleeper accounts born on one day. The campaign scenarios were written into the practice data so the tests could be checked; on real data the verdicts are whatever the data says.
